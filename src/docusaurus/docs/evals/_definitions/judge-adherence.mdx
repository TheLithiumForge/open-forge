Judge adherence says how well the work follows the spec, as a share of full marks. Judges see the project's rules quoted word for word and score four dimensions out of 10: whether the rules hold through the whole journey and not just the first turn, whether they're kept in intent and not only by the letter, whether every clause of the request is met soundly, and whether the new code respects the patterns the project already uses.

It's read beside check coverage, not mixed into it. Check coverage tests each rule on the paths the checks drive, and judges weigh what those paths don't reach.
