# Observations

Observations are agent-noticed grounded findings that help future agents learn from work.

## Axioms

- This `entrypoint` exposes grounded facts, signals, constraints, recurring behavior, risks, and evidence noticed during work.
- Read `Entries` when current work may depend on prior noticed findings.
- Before ending meaningful work, write observations for grounded findings that may matter later but are not accepted truth.
- If you identify a finding worth preserving, write the observation before final response or report the blocker.
- Treat observations as contextual until they are validated, promoted, or explicitly accepted.
- Keep source, scope, and uncertainty visible.
- Verify observations before treating them as current.
- Use observations as agent learning material until a route accepts, rejects, or supersedes them.
- Promote repeated, validated, or important observations to the route that owns the resulting material.
- That owner may be another #Memory route, a matching #Core route, an external system, or archived history.
- Archive stale, rejected, or disproven observations.
- Load only the observation bodies and child categories relevant to the current request.
- Add child categories when they improve routing, ownership, or clarity.
- Generated `entries` are navigation and reserved load policy only.
