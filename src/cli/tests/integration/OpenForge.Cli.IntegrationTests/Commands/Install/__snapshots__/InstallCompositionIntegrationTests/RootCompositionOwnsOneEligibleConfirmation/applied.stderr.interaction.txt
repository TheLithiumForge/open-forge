Where should Open Forge keep its context files? Press Enter for .agents, or
enter a new or existing workspace-relative folder. Settings and Skills stay in
.agents.
Do you also want Open Forge to maintain a folder of your own docs? Enter an
existing workspace-relative folder, such as docs, to register it as an outside
route, or press Enter to skip. It can't be inside the context folder or
.agents.
Choose your Open Forge setup

  1. Minimal     Recommended. Rules, accepted knowledge, your documents
     The loader, Directives for your rules and Crystallized Memory for accepted
     knowledge, plus the Open Forge sections in AGENTS.md and CLAUDE.md. Link
     documents you already have from the loader.
  2. Essentials  Minimal plus patterns, skills and working notes
     Everything in Minimal, plus reusable patterns, skills, notes for active
     work and findings not accepted yet. New notes for active work stay out of
     Git.
  3. Full Core   Every built-in folder, all shared through Git
     Everything in Essentials, plus advice, pointers to sources, starter files
     and archived records. All contents, including notes for active work, are
     shared through Git.
  4. Custom      Choose each folder on the next screen
     Starts from your current choices, or Essentials in a new workspace, then
     shows one list to change.
