# Orchestration Templates

## Axioms

- Use an Observation for a concrete occurrence worth retaining. It can come from a person, one agent, several contributors, or a tool; no Overseer is required.
- Use a Handoff for an actual transfer or explicitly planned resumption that needs a fixed snapshot while live state may continue to change. Routine pauses do not require one.
- Keep immediate progress and recovery details in the active working record. Preserve a separate Observation only when the evidence may prevent future cost or inform a reusable improvement.
- Evidence, recurrence, and reviewer agreement do not establish acceptance. Keep interpretations and proposed changes distinct from what happened.
- Use the workspace's existing record formats when suitable. Replace metadata and prompts, rebase links, and remove sections that do not help the next reader.
- Template copies belong to their destination. Updating or removing these starters does not change those copies.
