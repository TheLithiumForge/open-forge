# Fit And Risk Check

## Use When

Use when direction depends on whether the idea fits its audience, use case, market, constraints, or validation path.

## Capability

- Identify who benefits and why they would care.
- State the riskiest assumptions.
- Suggest cheap validation signals or questions.
- Separate product risk, technical risk, operational risk, and adoption risk when useful.

## Expected Result

The workflow can show what must be true for the direction to be worth pursuing.
