# Vision

Vision clarifies what should exist, why it matters, what belongs in the first useful version, and where it may grow later.

## Goal

- outcome: a vision with a core value, an MVP boundary, explicit non-goals, and later growth directions
- acceptance: the user accepts, revises, defers, or rejects the direction explicitly
- stop: the user accepts the direction, asks to defer, or rejects the work

## Required Routes

Read every route below before Step 1. A route that cannot be read is a blocker to report, not a step to skip.

- `.agents/skills/workflow-primitives/SKILL.md` - shared context check, memory routing, completion, and handoff primitives
- `.agents/skills/vision/SKILL.md` - vision shaping, MVP boundary, fit, risk, and growth-direction capability

## Constraints

- Treat the vision as candidate direction until the user accepts it.
- Do not promote strategic direction to #CurrentTruth without user confirmation.
- Prefer concise questions and concrete options over broad strategy prose.

## Steps

1. Clarify the problem, audience, desired outcome, constraints, and non-goals.
2. Identify the core value that must exist for the direction to matter.
3. Shape the MVP boundary and separate later growth directions.
4. Check fit, risks, validation signals, and assumptions that could invalidate the direction.
5. Ask the user to accept, revise, defer, or reject the direction.
6. Route accepted truth, candidate ideas, and follow-up context to the right #Memory routes.

## Loop

Iterative: repeat steps 1 through 5 when the user changes the audience, core value, MVP boundary, constraints, risks, or validation path. Stop per the Goal's stop condition.

## Outputs

- accepted or candidate vision summary
- MVP boundary and explicit non-goals
- future directions
- risks, assumptions, and validation signals
- open questions

## Completion

- [ ] accepted truth and candidate ideas routed to the right #Memory routes
