# Vision

Vision clarifies what should exist, why it matters, what belongs in the first useful version, and where it may grow later.

## Goal

- outcome: a vision with a core value, a first-useful-version boundary, explicit non-goals, and later growth directions
- acceptance: the requested candidate analysis is delivered with its status explicit, or the user accepts, revises, defers, or rejects the direction
- stop: requested analysis delivered, explicit direction decision made, required context unavailable, or user input needed for a value choice

## Required Routes

Read every route below before Step 1. A route that cannot be read is a blocker to report, not a step to skip.

- `.agents/skills/vision/SKILL.md` - vision shaping, first-useful-version boundary, fit, risk, and growth-direction capability

## Constraints

- Treat the vision as candidate direction until the user accepts it.
- Do not promote strategic direction to #CurrentTruth without user confirmation.
- Prefer concise questions and concrete options over broad strategy prose.

## Steps

1. Clarify the problem, audience, desired outcome, constraints, and non-goals.
2. Identify the core value that must exist for the direction to matter.
3. Shape the first useful version and separate later growth directions.
4. Check fit, risks, validation signals, and assumptions that could invalidate the direction.
5. When the user requested a decision or the direction may become project truth, ask them to accept, revise, defer, or reject it; otherwise present it explicitly as a candidate with the next decision or validation need.
6. Route accepted truth, warranted candidate ideas, and follow-up context to the right #Memory routes only when that mutation is safe and authorized; otherwise report the proposed destination or that no durable routing is warranted.

## Loop

Iterative: repeat steps 1 through 5 when the user changes the audience, core value, first-useful-version boundary, constraints, risks, or validation path. Stop per the Goal's stop condition.

## Outputs

- accepted or candidate vision summary
- first-useful-version boundary and explicit non-goals
- future directions
- risks, assumptions, and validation signals
- open questions
