# Design Review

Design Review produces prioritized experience findings and an acceptance recommendation without modifying the design by default.

## Mode

iterative

## Goal

- outcome: evidence-backed design findings, strengths, unresolved questions, and a clear accept, revise, or validate recommendation
- acceptance: the review covers the intended journey and relevant states, with each issue tied to user impact and correction guidance
- stop: the design, user need, intended platform, or critical states cannot be inspected

## Required Routes

Read every route below before Step 1. A route that cannot be read is a blocker to report, not a step to skip.

- [experience criteria, state coverage, accessibility review, and implementation feasibility](../../skills/experience-design/SKILL.md) - #Skill #Design #UX

## Constraints

- Review is read-only unless design revisions are explicitly requested.
- Distinguish missing evidence from a confirmed design defect.
- Evaluate the whole journey and state transitions, not isolated screens alone.
- Prioritize user harm, blocked completion, accessibility, destructive mistakes, and unrecoverable states over taste.

## Steps

1. Establish target users, intended outcome, success signal, platform constraints, and the design artifact or behavior under review.
2. Trace the primary journey and applicable alternate, empty, loading, error, permission, interruption, and recovery states.
3. Evaluate discoverability, comprehension, hierarchy, feedback, control, efficiency, accessibility, consistency, and trust.
4. Check content and interactions against established product language, patterns, technical constraints, and accepted decisions.
5. Identify strengths worth preserving and issues with user impact, evidence, severity, and correction guidance.
6. Challenge findings for unsupported preference, missing context, and mitigations already present.
7. Recommend accept, revise, or validate, and name the smallest evidence needed for unresolved questions.
8. If revisions were explicitly requested, keep the original findings distinct from the proposed revisions.

## Loop

Repeat steps 2 through 7 when new states, evidence, or revisions materially change the assessment. Stop when the design is ready for a decision or missing context blocks a defensible review.

## Outputs

- strengths and prioritized experience findings
- journey and state coverage gaps
- accessibility, consistency, and feasibility risks
- accept, revise, or validate recommendation
- unresolved questions and evidence needed

## Completion

- [ ] primary journey and relevant states were reviewed
- [ ] findings state user impact, evidence, and correction guidance
- [ ] preference is separated from usability or accessibility risk
- [ ] the design remained unchanged unless revisions were explicitly requested
- [ ] final response or handoff names this workflow
