# Phase 4 - Blue

## Goal

Inspect the green production structure in one bounded improvement pass and make a material improvement to clarity, locality, boundaries, duplication, or measured performance when justified, while preserving the frozen contract and green evidence.

## Steps

1. Read the Task, frozen contract, Green handoff, accepted evidence, source-structure rules, accepted architecture, allowed production paths, and forbidden surfaces supplied by the Mastermind.
2. Confirm the selected focused evidence is green before structural change.
3. Review names, module focus, navigability, crowded flat folders, cohesive subfolder and namespace opportunities, responsibility locality, duplication, error handling, dependency direction, resource ownership, excessive parameter plumbing, broad or artificial context bags, nested or chained conditional expressions, repeated finite policy, and measured hot paths where they matter to the Task. Avoid both a large flat catalogue and one-file microfolders.
4. Make only material structural improvements within accepted architecture. Keep files focused and prefer direct readable composition over abstraction without demonstrated consumers. Prefer one or two parameters and normally no more than four or five for behavioral call surfaces, passing a real cohesive stage record rather than unpacking and repacking it or inventing an `Args` bag. When an accepted shared policy or multiple real consumers repeatedly select finite behavior, prefer one typed immutable static strategy table over duplicated branching or a class hierarchy. If the inspection exposes a material architecture or behavior problem, return it to the Mastermind instead of changing behavior here.
5. Run focused evidence throughout, then the selected toolchain checks and scoped tests after the final change. The public scenario and full gate remain Mastermind-owned cycle steps.
6. Return the structural changes, before-and-after evidence, and consciously deferred improvements. When inspection finds no material improvement, return that conclusion with its evidence and make no change.
7. After integrated inspection, when Blue changes files, the Mastermind updates
   the authoritative Task progress in the same coherent commit and commits the
   accepted Blue phase before Purple mutates files. That Blue commit is separate
   from Green. When Blue makes no change, its evidence is recorded in the next
   coherent phase or acceptance commit and no empty commit is created. When Blue changes files,
   the Mastermind compares Purple's starting tree with the Blue commit. When
   Blue reports no material change, Purple starts from the most recent
   mutating-phase commit plus the recorded Blue result. In either case, the
   Mastermind verifies that the contract and tests are protected.

Do not change public behavior, callable contracts, tests, fixtures, snapshots, accepted expectations, Task state, or unrelated surfaces, and do not stage or commit phase work. Do not discover behavior, add speculative abstractions, or extend the pass for cosmetic preference. The Mastermind alone performs the Task update and phase commit at the boundary below.

## Completion

- The implementation is materially clearer, simpler, or safer than the Green version, or bounded inspection found no material structural defect and produced no change.
- The frozen contract, accepted behavior, and green evidence are unchanged.
- Selected focused checks remain green after the final structural result.
- The Mastermind inspected the actual Blue paths, diff, and evidence, recorded
  Task progress, and committed a separate accepted Blue phase before Purple
  continued, unless Blue made no change.
- No speculative abstraction, unrelated cleanup, or preference-only change entered this one Blue pass.
