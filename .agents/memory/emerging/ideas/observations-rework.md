# Distributed Observation Consolidation

## Current Accepted Boundary

The current Observation contract already accepts the durable parts of the
original rework:

- Not every task produces an Observation.
- One grounded occurrence is enough when it is plausibly reusable, surprising,
  or costly to rediscover.
- Evidence, source, scope, and uncertainty remain visible.
- Recurrence raises the case for consolidation and promotion instead of being
  a prerequisite for capture.
- Observations remain contextual until an authoritative source accepts,
  rejects, or replaces their outcome.

## Residual Opportunity

Distributed work can still produce several isolated reports about the same
subject without any agent seeing the recurrence. Explore a cheap search and
append path that can recognize a matching Observation, preserve distinct
occurrence evidence, and surface a consolidation or promotion candidate.

The support must not:

- Make tools authoritative for semantic equivalence.
- Reintroduce a per-task Observation obligation.
- Merge merely similar findings that have different causes, scope, or meaning.
- Promote a Directive, Pattern, Decision, or current document automatically.
- Convert explicit user ideas into Observations instead of recording them in
  the matching candidate route.

## Evidence Before Promotion

Seed several independent occurrences with known same-subject and near-match
cases. Verify that unfamiliar agents can find the prior material, append only
compatible evidence, preserve divergent findings, and propose the correct
durable role without excessive ceremony.
