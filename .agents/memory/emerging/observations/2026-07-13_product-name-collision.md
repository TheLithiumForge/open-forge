# Observation: The Methodology Name Is More Defensible Than The Current Product Brand

Date: 2026-07-13. Source: lightweight public-web collision screening during the framework-assessment revision. This is not trademark, package, domain, corporate-name, or legal clearance.

## Findings

- `Context-Routed Development` did not surface as an established exact methodology phrase in the screening and remains a precise category description.
- Its acronym `CRD` is strongly occupied by Kubernetes `CustomResourceDefinition`; public positioning should spell out the phrase.
- `Context-Driven Development` is already active language in agentic software writing. Adaptive Software Development, evolutionary development, and living documentation are established categories that would import misleading expectations.
- `OpenForge` has a direct same-market collision: GitGuardian publishes an OpenForge platform for AI agent plugins and skills. Other active Open Forge/OpenForge products compound searchability risk.
- Qualifying the current name with the methodology improves explanation but does not remove search, package, or legal confusion.
- `Contextive`, `Contexture`, and `Contextweave` are occupied in adjacent context, DDD, or AI-tool territory. `Seedwork` already presents an LLM-native cultivation methodology; `Grove Method` and `GROVE` also have current ITSM and AI uses.
- `Stigmergic` is a technically useful description of repository traces shaping later work, but it is active agent-research vocabulary rather than an ownable public category.
- `Routecology` and `Routogenesis` produced no obvious exact software-method collision in the lightweight screen. `Contexticulture` produced obscure older academic appearances rather than a current AI methodology. These results justify further testing, not a claim of availability.

## Candidate Action

- Keep the methodology and product-name decisions separate: use Context-Routed Development as the candidate category, and run a deliberate brand-clearance/rebrand decision before public launch investment.
- Use a layered stack: `Context-Routed Development` as the formal methodology, `Routed Context Cultivation` as the optional organic umbrella, `organic growth, deliberate promotion` as philosophy, and `Route → Work → Verify → Cultivate` as the lifecycle loop.
- Preserve `Routecology` as the leading frontier-field experiment. Require comprehension, false-expectation, recall, pronunciation, collision, legal, package, domain, and multilingual checks before public adoption.
- Treat suggested replacement product names as creative directions only until legal, registry, domain, and user-confusion checks are complete.

Suggested promotion destination after maintainer decision: product-direction decision and user-facing README positioning.
