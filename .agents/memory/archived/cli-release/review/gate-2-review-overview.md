review queue, or current authority. It records the CLI review history through the
settled Queue 33 rejection. No command review is active.

## Historical review use

The former [review queue](queue.md) preserves settled disposition and review order.
It records the completed review order, rejected or superseded Queue 30 history,
and the contextual Queue 31–33 records. Read a packet for its origin, constraints,
recommendation, dissent, choices, and consequences. Validate any historical idea
against current direction before restoring or reusing it.

Groundwork in these records may include repository analysis, independent council
perspectives, factual reconciliation, synthesis, and focused rebuttal. It is
historical evidence, not an implementation, Architecture, or shipping claim.
