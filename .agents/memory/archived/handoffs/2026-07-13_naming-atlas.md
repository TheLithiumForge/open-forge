## Goal

Expand the existing Open Forge assessment with a much broader naming exploration for the methodology category. Cover natural-flow, routable/growing context, human-like cognition, and deliberately frontier or coined directions while preserving the existing product/methodology/philosophy/loop separation.

## Completed Outcome

- Preserved `Context-Routed Development` as the recommended formal methodology.
- Added `Routed Context Cultivation` as the recommended broader organic practice and retained `organic growth, deliberate promotion` as the philosophy.
- Added more than one hundred constructions across route, minimal/on-demand, natural-flow, organic, human-governance, cognition, evidence, repo-native, coined, and mnemonic families.
- Scored twenty serious candidates and modeled twenty-four frontier candidates in reproducible SQL.
- Identified `Routecology` as the leading frontier-field experiment, with `Contexticulture` and `Routogenesis` as secondary wildcards.
- Clarified that `androgynous` is unrelated to human-like behavior; `human-governed`, `human-stewarded`, `cognition-scaffolded`, and `endogenous` carry distinct useful meanings.
- Expanded public collision screening without representing it as legal clearance.
- Revised, validated, and rendered the complete MCP technical report rather than creating a naming-only fragment.
