# Provisional Refactor Directions

These directions came from the first roadmap synthesis. They are preserved as historical context after their useful open work moved to focused destinations.

## Resolution

- Workflow simplification, selection, dependencies, and native-capability boundaries moved through the archived [Workflow Overhaul Inputs](2026-07-29_workflow-overhaul-inputs.md) into the current [Workflow contract](../../crystallized/documents/framework/primitives/workflows.md).
- Extension packaging, catalogue governance, and lifecycle reconsideration moved to [Extensions Overhaul](../../emerging/ideas/extensions-overhaul.md).
- Optional structured deliberation moved to [Deferred Product Ideas](../../emerging/ideas/deferred-product-ideas.md).
- Remaining documentation, hardening, and release work remain in the [working backlog](../../working/backlog.md).
- The historical `team-development` Extension and its modes were not revived without new evidence.

Candidate directions to reassess:

- make workflows optional and replace the legacy eight-section schema with a smaller contract;
- preserve top-down workflow selection and the non-waterfall phase vocabulary where they still earn their cost;
- consolidate generic development workflows and remove generic skill dependencies that duplicate native agent competence;
- reconsider the `team-development` extension, its shared timing behavior, and its Vision, Architecture, Planning, Development, Debugging, and Review modes;
- audit the extension catalogue before retiring or merging existing packages;
- evaluate an optional deliberation or Decision Congress capability;
- rewrite stale product and extension documentation after the accepted product shape is known;
- run final consistency, portability, packaging, and behavioral hardening after the roadmap stabilizes.

The focused destinations above contain any remaining future work.
