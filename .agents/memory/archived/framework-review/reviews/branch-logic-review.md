### Explicit Rules And Their Consequences

1. **Authority is tied to role and scope.** `src/open-forge/.agents/loader.md:25` says each source answers one question and links do not transfer authority, scope, loading, or lifecycle. Lines 26–30 distinguish binding Axioms/Directives, scoped default replacement, additive instructions, acceptance, and current-truth conflicts. The category questions and answers agree with these boundaries. A narrower binding instruction cannot silently defeat a parent instruction; a narrower non-binding source may specialize the same kind of material.
2. **Loading follows exposed, selected routes.** Loader lines 43–57 define ancestor-first routing, optional scopes, removable defaults, and immediately adjacent overwrites. Lines 63–72 expressly prohibit LoadNow or KeepInMind from activating an unselected ancestor. KeepInMind adds refresh timing within active scopes. Entries provide navigation; an overwrite is interpreted within the base source's role and scope. These rules do not grant an overwrite unrelated authority.
3. **Evidence, acceptance, placement, and integration are separate.** Loader line 29 separates validation from acceptance. `src/open-forge/.agents/memory/_memory.md:19` prevents a record from acquiring another category's role by recording its content. Lines 25–45 require deliberate durable capture and integration into the source that should define each outcome. Crystallized Memory names sources of acceptance; tags, repetition, and agent confidence cannot supply it. Working Memory may preserve an accepted temporary choice with source, scope, and expiration.
4. **Current explanation and rationale complement one another.** The Documents route requires usable current meaning without reconstructing it from Decisions. Decisions keep the accepted choice and useful rationale, and link to current specifications. Neither rule requires a separate document for every fact or a Decision for every routine choice.
5. **Archiving includes never-accepted material.** `src/open-forge/.agents/memory/archived/_archived.md:11` defines history by its lack of current control, without requiring prior acceptance. Lines 16–21 require extraction of still-current content, useful provenance, removal of metadata that retains the former role or current authority, proportionate retention, authorized deletion, and validation plus acceptance before current reuse. Handoff sealing lasts while the record serves that transfer; its useful remainder may then be archived under destination rules.
6. **Patterns, Templates, and Workflows remain distinct.** Patterns supply reusable default shapes, with deliberate departures explained before reliance and existing authority used for exceptions to agreed shapes. Templates supply independently maintained starting content and require replacement of copied metadata. Workflows are optional recipes selected for value, with complete Goal/Steps/Completion sections; installed content is not a mandatory sequence. Native Skills follow their own resources and runtime activation rules.
