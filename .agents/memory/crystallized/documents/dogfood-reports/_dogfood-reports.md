# Dogfood Reports

Dogfood reports are accepted records of evidence and analysis from testing Open Forge against real agent sessions.

## Axioms

- Read `Entries` when current work needs dogfood evidence, recommendation history, or detailed reasoning behind loading and routing decisions.
- Treat report bodies as records, not active behavior or automatically accepted recommendations.
- Prefer crystallized decisions for accepted conclusions and policy; load reports when evidence, source reasoning, or recommendation history matters.
- Keep new accepted dogfood evidence or recommendation records here after extracting accepted conclusions to their owning routes.
