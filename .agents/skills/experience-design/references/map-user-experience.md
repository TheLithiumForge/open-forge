# Map User Experience

## Criteria

- Establish the target users, job, evidence, constraints, success signal, entry conditions, and exit conditions.
- Map the primary journey and only the alternate, empty, loading, success, error, permission, interruption, destructive, and recovery states that can materially affect it.
- Show triggers, transitions, feedback, persistence, cancellation, and recovery rather than treating screens as isolated artifacts.
- Compare distinct directions by comprehension, effort, control, accessibility, consistency, feasibility, reversibility, and risk.
- Recommend from evidence. When evidence is insufficient, define the smallest prototype, research question, or decision that can resolve the uncertainty.

## Result

The experience directions cover the meaningful journey and states without turning candidate behavior into accepted requirements.
