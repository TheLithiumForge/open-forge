# Experience Design

Carry user intent through the complete experience without presenting candidates, missing evidence, or implementation choices as accepted design.

## Method

- Start from users, jobs, evidence, constraints, and success signals, not screens alone.
- Trace journeys and transitions across normal, empty, loading, error, permission, interruption, destructive, and recovery states that apply.
- Evaluate comprehension, discoverability, feedback, control, efficiency, accessibility, consistency, feasibility, and trust.
- Keep observations, inferences, candidates, accepted decisions, and implementation choices separate.
- Match the artifact's detail to the decision. Do not invent missing content, assets, platform behavior, or user evidence.
