# Experience Design

Carry user intent through the complete experience without turning candidates, missing evidence, or implementation discretion into accepted design truth.

## Method

- Start from users, jobs, evidence, constraints, and success signals rather than screens alone.
- Trace journeys and transitions across normal, empty, loading, error, permission, interruption, destructive, and recovery states that apply.
- Evaluate comprehension, discoverability, feedback, control, efficiency, accessibility, consistency, feasibility, and trust.
- Keep observations, inferences, candidates, accepted decisions, and implementation discretion distinct.
- Match artifact fidelity to the decision and never invent missing content, assets, platform behavior, or user evidence.
