# CLI Dogfooding Evidence

## Instructions

- Treat creation and use of the replacement Open Forge CLI as Framework dogfooding. Preserve evidence not only about product behavior, but also about whether routing, Tasks, phase agents, delegation packets, review roles, workflows, generated projections, tests, and verification mechanisms help or hinder the work.
- Record a concrete occurrence when an agent fails to start or finish, reaches a limit, is underused, duplicates work, crosses scope, produces an unexpected structure, omits a useful improvement, or needs Mastermind correction; when a maintainer changes or repeats a direction after inspecting the result; when a command, tool, generated projection, or workflow behaves unexpectedly; or when the Mastermind must take over work that a bounded role was expected to perform.
- Keep immediate operational detail in the active CLI Task or checkpoint. Create or extend a matching Emerging Observation when the occurrence may prevent future cost, support comparison, refine an agent or workflow, or become a reusable Pattern. Record expected behavior, observed behavior, consequence, recovery, reviewer or maintainer reasoning, alternatives and tradeoffs, and the smallest future signal that would justify promotion or rejection.
- Preserve longitudinal comparisons without biasing independent first passes. After a reviewer returns, compare its sanitized rationale with earlier matching observations and append meaningful agreement, disagreement, repeated options, and changed evidence.
- Do not record provider, model, AI, runtime-profile, session, task, review, handoff, or hidden orchestration identifiers; personal, user, or machine identifiers; secrets or tokens; local absolute paths; or incidental environment fingerprints. Use role names, repository-relative paths, normalized commands only when needed, and behavior-level failure descriptions.
- Observations remain contextual evidence. Recurrence does not silently change Framework authority. Promote only the smallest supported rule, Pattern, Workflow, agent instruction, or implementation after the evidence and maintainer acceptance justify it.
