# Framework Essence

## Axioms

- Keep baseline context and mandatory rules as small as possible; every always-loaded addition must earn its ongoing attention cost.
- Maximize flexible composition through plain-file routes and native agent primitives instead of hidden orchestration or special-case machinery.
- Use memory to preserve continuity and learning, while treating promotion into normative truth as a deliberate human-governed choice.
- Design structure and deterministic tools to increase the probability of good results from non-deterministic agents; do not claim that they mechanically guarantee compliant behavior.
- Prefer one consistent top-to-bottom routing pattern over repeated local decisions, and correct dogfood, source payload, documentation, and tests when they contradict the chosen pattern.
