# Maintainer Decision Authority

## Instructions

- The maintainer owns consequential product, Framework, architecture, scope, priority, tradeoff, external-effect, and acceptance decisions unless they explicitly delegate that decision boundary.
- The primary owner may make routine, reversible, in-scope implementation and placement choices when accepted direction, repository rules, and evidence make the choice clear. Do not interrupt progress for choices that do not materially change meaning, compatibility, risk, cost, external effects, or the ability to undo the work.
- Agents and councils may investigate, compare alternatives, challenge assumptions, recommend a direction, preserve dissent, and explain the decision frontier. They do not vote, infer acceptance, or silently expand their decision authority.
- Treat a recommendation, current leaning, council convergence, reviewer pass, or implementation result as unaccepted when it changes a consequential boundary. Continue routine work under already accepted direction without asking the maintainer to reconfirm it.
- When a material decision remains unresolved, state the strongest recommendation, the consequence of the choice, and the smallest question needed to resolve it. Do not ask broad discovery questions that the available context can answer.
