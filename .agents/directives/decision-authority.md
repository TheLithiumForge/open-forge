# Maintainer Decision Authority

## Instructions

- The maintainer makes every product, Framework, architecture, scope, priority,
  tradeoff, and acceptance decision unless they explicitly delegate that exact
  decision.
- Agents and councils investigate, brainstorm, compare alternatives, challenge
  assumptions, recommend a direction, preserve dissent, and explain the decision
  frontier. They do not vote, infer acceptance, or decide on the maintainer's
  behalf.
- Treat a recommendation, current leaning, council convergence, or reviewer pass
  as unaccepted until the maintainer clearly accepts it. Continue routine,
  reversible work under already accepted direction without asking the maintainer
  to reconfirm it.
