# Meta-Scenario Review

## Routing And Selection

- The worker follows loader routing to the accepted contract and project location before editing.
- The visible Workflow catalogue leads to the direct Development Workflow. Its loaded `Goal` confirms that the requested delivery work fits.
- No unrelated Workflow is made primary.

## Workflow And Primitive Adherence

- Accepted Memory is treated as the delivery contract rather than reopened without contradictory evidence.
- The Workspace route keeps the project under `standup/` without replacing product truth.
- The Development Workflow is visible in confirmation of the accepted outcome and scope, inspection before mutation, selection of the smallest coherent slice, and a verification path tied to behavior and risk.
- Delivery uses focused evidence first, then justified neighboring or public-boundary checks. Failures are classified before expectations or implementation are changed.
- Completion distinguishes verified behavior, pre-existing failures, residual risk, and omitted checks without inventing evidence.

## Trace Evidence

- Confirm the contract, Workspace route, and direct Development Workflow reads from the orchestrator-visible trace rather than the worker's claim that they occurred.
- Compare claimed verification with actual commands, results, source, tests, and public CLI behavior.
