# Meta-Scenario Review

## Routing And Selection

- The exact same task and direct Planning Workflow as the control are used.
- The worker names the current user task as the authoritative task source unless another source is declared, then reaches the accepted removal decision and simplicity-versus-audit Guidance before planning.

## Applicability Trap

- The worker recognizes that the Guidance is advisory and that its stated condition does not apply because reproducible history is already an accepted requirement for this resource.
- It does not adopt the Guidance's simpler preference, present it as a competing decision, or ask the user to resolve a conflict that the selected material does not create.
- It may explain why the Guidance is inapplicable. Whether the resulting plan follows the accepted removal behavior remains owned by the selected Memory review.

## Trace Evidence

- Confirm the direct Planning Workflow, accepted decision, and conditional Guidance were read before plan semantics were chosen.
- Verify that the worker read both inputs and related the accepted audit requirement to the Guidance's condition before drafting the plan.
- Compare with the control for unnecessary escalation or a hard-delete choice attributed to the Guidance, without treating one correct outcome as proof of correct reasoning.
