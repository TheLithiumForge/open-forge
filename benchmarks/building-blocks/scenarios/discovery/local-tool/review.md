# Scenario Review

## Behavior

- Questions are concise and materially clarify pain, capture friction, retrieval timing, privacy, project attribution, temporal boundaries, prior failure, and non-goals.
- The time-tracking suggestion is probed for its underlying need before scope changes.
- Git is assessed as incomplete evidence rather than accepted as the primary record.
- Discovery remains discovery; no implementation begins and uncertainty is not presented as settled fact.

## Outcome

- The accepted direction includes fast local terminal capture, project-grouped recall, Monday covering Friday through Sunday, and the stated privacy and scope boundaries.
- Accepted direction, unresolved choices, and worthwhile later ideas remain visibly distinct.
- The result is small enough to be a credible first useful version.
